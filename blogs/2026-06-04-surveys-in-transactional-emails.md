Buttondown has fixed a bug preventing surveys from displaying in transactional emails like welcome messages. Users can now embed surveys in welcome, confirmation, gift, churn, and premium-confirmation emails using the same slash-command method as regular emails, with responses recorded once per subscriber. The fix is live automatically for every newsletter with surveys enabled.
